About Our Brands

Bella+Canvas bellacanvas.com

Founded in 1998 and based in Los Angeles, Bella+Canvas is the go-to brand for retail-quality blanks with a modern, fashion-forward fit. Their tees are known for buttery-soft Airlume combed and ring-spun cotton, slim fits, and one of the widest color and style ranges in the industry. Manufactured ethically (FLA Accredited, WRAP Certified) with sustainable practices including bluesign approved dyes and pre-shrunk fabric for minimal shrinkage. Best for brands that want a contemporary, lightweight feel and a fitted silhouette. Note that styles run a bit slim, so size up for a relaxed look.

Comfort Colors comfortcolors.com

Started in Vermont in 1975 and now part of Gildan (acquired 2015), Comfort Colors pioneered the garment-dyed style. Heavyweight 100% ring-spun cotton tees, tanks, and fleece with a lived-in softness, relaxed fit, and a vintage, weathered look right out of the bag. Famous for a deep palette of nature-inspired pigment-dyed colors that fade beautifully over time. Hugely popular with college bookstores, screen printers, and lifestyle brands wanting a soft-washed, retro feel. Heavier weight and a looser cut than Bella+Canvas. Note that some color and length variation is normal due to the dye process.

Independent Trading Co. independenttradingco.com

Southern California-based, founded in 1987 with roots in action sports. The undisputed leader in blank fleece, especially heavyweight hoodies and crewnecks. The flagship IND4000 is considered a streetwear industry standard, and the line spans lightweight to super-heavyweight options including pigment-dyed, tie-dyed, special blend, and premium cross-grain styles. Known for solid construction, quality fleece, and consistency across runs. Best choice when hoodies, sweatshirts, joggers, or jackets are the focus rather than tees.

Stanley/Stella stanleystella.com

Brussels-based and founded in 2012, Stanley/Stella is the premium sustainability play. Every garment is made from 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton or recycled polyester, ranking among the top five organic cotton buyers globally. Fair Wear Foundation certified production with full supply chain traceability. The line covers tees, hoodies, sweats, and outerwear in modern European fits, varied weights (155 to 350 GSM), and an evolving on-trend color palette. Engineered for clean print and DTG performance. Best for eco-conscious brands and premium merch where sustainability is part of the story. Sizing tends to run a bit relaxed compared to US brands.

How This Store Works

Products are managed in a Notion database and printed on-demand through Printful at production cost. No retail markup. Orders are routed to store admins for approval before the company card is charged.

To Add a New Product

  1. Log in to Printful Product Templates and create a new template. Match brand colors — logos are already loaded in the portal.
  2. Once the template is created, copy its share link.
  3. Log in to the Notion Product Database (use 3dsourceteam@gmail.com), or request access via the link.
  4. Add a new row: paste the Printful URL, fill in Title, Style, Type, and Description, upload a product photo, and check Active.
  5. The store updates automatically within 5 minutes.

Purchasing

All items are sold at production cost. When a team member places an order, it routes to Printful store admins for approval before being charged to the company card.

Need Access?

Ask to be added as an admin to both the Notion workspace and the Printful store.
